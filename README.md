# The Childhood Myth Index

**Exploring myths, facts, and patterns in statements about child development**

A beginner-friendly exploratory data analysis (EDA) project that investigates how statements about child development are classified as facts or myths, how strong those classifications are, and whether facts and myths differ in length.

##  About the Project

The Childhood Myth Index examines a publicly available dataset of statements about child development, each labeled as a fact or a myth with an associated confidence/strength level (Strong, Moderate, Weak, or Hardly).

Rather than collecting new survey data, this project works directly with an existing dataset — cleaning it, analyzing it, and visualizing the patterns found within it.

---

##  Questions We Asked

- Are there more facts or myths in the dataset?
- How are facts and myths distributed across different strength levels?
- What percentage of statements are myths?
- Are fact statements longer than myth statements?
- Does the proportion of myths change depending on classification strength?

---

##  Dataset

- **Source size:** 1,397 statements about child development
- **After cleaning:** 1,392 statements (5 exact duplicate rows removed)

The original dataset combined type and strength into a single label, e.g. "Strong Fact" or "Moderate Myth." These were split into two separate variables for analysis:

| Variable | Values |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Fact, Myth |
| **Strength** | Strong, Moderate, Weak, Hardly |

---

##  Key Findings

### Facts vs. Myths

| Type | Share |
|---|---:|
| Facts | 68.61% |
| Myths | 31.39% |

### Strength Distribution

**Among fact statements:**

| Strength | Share |
|---|---:|
| Strong | 72.25% |
| Weak | 14.66% |
| Moderate | 12.46% |
| Hardly | 0.63% |

**Among myth statements:**

| Strength | Share |
|---|---:|
| Strong | 59.73% |
| Moderate | 30.89% |
| Weak | 9.38% |

### Myth Percentage by Strength

| Strength | % Myths |
|---|---:|
| Moderate | **53.15%** |
| Strong | 27.44% |
| Weak | 22.65% |
| Hardly | 0.00% |

Statements labeled "Moderate" are the only strength category where myths make up more than half of the statements — myths are noticeably over-represented here compared to the Strong and Weak categories.

### Statement Length

| Measure | Facts | Myths |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average words | 24.12 | 10.35 |
| Median words | 20 | 9 |

Fact statements are roughly **twice as long**, on average, as myth statements in this dataset.

## Limitations

- This dataset is not a random sample of all childhood beliefs or child-development claims, so findings describe **patterns within this dataset only** and shouldn't be generalized.
- The analysis identifies associations and patterns, not causal relationships.
